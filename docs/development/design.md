# Floralia interface

Direction recorded 2026-11-06: a quiet editorial workshop. The working specimen is the central surface; controls live in an open right rail. Two generated full-screen concepts establish the Page garden and Shape & letter states. The UI is HTML/CSS; all botanical output is generated by the engines, not a raster mockup.

Tokens: paper `#f7f6f2`, ink `#282d27`, olive `#626b4d`, hairline `#d4d5cb`, white artboard. Georgia provides editorial serif headings and an italic wordmark; system sans is used for compact, readable controls. The bundled IM Fell DW Pica font provides the generated serif letter outlines. Square fields and buttons, thin rules, no card stacks, no gradients.

Desktop: brand row, two numbered tabs, specimen workspace and roughly 320px inspector, thin footer. Mobile: the specimen precedes the inspector, with no fixed height or horizontal scrolling. Page controls include reproducible seed, density, colors, season, visitor permissions and editable reserved rectangle. Shape controls include presets, lettering/font selection and custom drawing, density, flower size, colors and cell inspection. Shared actions generate a variation, export vector SVG, and copy a configuration link.

Intentional concept deviations: live algorithm output replaces the illustrative concept flowers; visitor controls say 'allowed' because upstream seeds determine arrivals; useful status/error feedback and numeric rectangle position controls supplement the concept. Actual generation capabilities and accessible interaction take precedence over decorative placeholder copy.

The two screens share one masthead, palette component and action treatment. The shape specimen keeps its actual square coordinate space instead of stretching to the concept's landscape frame; the A4 specimen keeps its physical aspect ratio. Outline and cell controls live in the inspector, with automatic fitting instead of a separate Fit button. Viewport-aware specimen sizing keeps the primary actions visible on a 1536 × 1024 desktop; smaller screens scroll naturally. Compact control text is 12–14px, while helper text is 11px. The initial page rectangle is 108 × 189mm, an asymmetric frame chosen for the real generator.
