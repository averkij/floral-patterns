## Our translation into experiments

- Offer a skeleton-only view and a flat silhouette view before showing texture. Judge the arrangement in both.
- Define a motif family with shared curvature and proportions, then vary a few bounded parameters. Compare it with independently randomized parts.
- Compare equal-sized flowers with a three-scale composition using the same total painted area.
- Build a repeat inspector showing a 3-by-3 tile neighborhood; inspect edge continuity and unwanted stripes.
- Keep an explicit empty-space control. Maximum coverage need not be the artistic objective.

These are proposed operational interpretations. For practical vocabulary on joints, distribution and balance, continue with Ward in the [reference trail](references.md).
