# Reference trail from Wong, Zongker and Salesin (1998)

Reviewed 2026-11-06. The numbering below preserves all 36 references from the paper's bibliography, on PDF pages 7-9. Bibliographic facts were checked against the [University of Washington copy](https://grail.cs.washington.edu/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/Wong98.pdf#page=7); spelling has been normalized where obvious. Years here identify the editions cited by the paper, not necessarily first publication.

**Review levels:** `selected text` means relevant passages were inspected; `abstract` means only an abstract/opening summary was reviewed; `catalog` means a primary collection record was inspected; `indexed` means recorded from Wong et al., without reviewing the original. None implies an exhaustive literature review.
