# Computer-Generated Floral Ornament

Michael T. Wong, Douglas E. Zongker and David H. Salesin. SIGGRAPH 1998, pp. 423-434. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/280814.280948), [requested ACM PDF](https://dl.acm.org/doi/epdf/10.1145/280814.280948), [University of Washington PDF reviewed](https://grail.cs.washington.edu/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/Wong98.pdf).

The ACM PDF endpoint was inaccessible during review; the university-hosted paper supplied the text, figures and bibliography. Reviewed 2026-11-06.

## Source findings

The paper treats ornament as rules that adapt to a region, separating layout geometry from rendering. Its scope is floral panels, not a general solution to seamless tiling or strict symmetry (sections 1-3).

Elements have simple collision proxies and richer renderers. Procedural rules query the environment and propose children; failed proposals are discarded. The controller grows toward an approximately largest eligible empty circle, trying nearby elements in distance order. A low-resolution buffer and Manhattan medial-axis transform supply candidate centers; circle inflation refines them. Circles stopped by the region boundary are discarded. Failed locations enter an exclusion list until nearby changes permit reconsideration (section 4, figure 8).

Design principles include repetition with variation, balanced masses, tangential connections, and adaptation to boundaries. Large-to-small filling can create hierarchy, but the willow example exposes weak global planning: good coverage can still look disorganized (sections 2 and 5, figure 11). Figure 17 renders one structure in several styles.

## Experiments we propose

- Build a deliberately simple circle-and-curve baseline before adding detailed flowers.
- Show accepted and rejected proposals, target discs and attachment choices in a step debugger.
- Compare the nearest parent with a shortlist scored for bending and continuation of existing movement.
- Measure final painted overlap separately from proxy overlap; stroke width and decoration can escape a proxy.
- Retain a fixed gallery of seeds so changes can be judged side by side.

These are implementation proposals, not a reproduction claim. See [experiments](../development/experiments.md). All numbered citations in the paper are indexed in the [reference trail](references.md).
